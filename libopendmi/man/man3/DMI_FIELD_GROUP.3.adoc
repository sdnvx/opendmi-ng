== Name

DMI_FIELD_GROUP, DMI_FIELD_OFFSET - declare where a structure may end and where its parts begin.

== Library

OpenDMI C library (`libopendmi`, `-lopendmi`)
endif::[]

== Synopsis

[source, c]
----
#include <opendmi/field.h>

DMI_FIELD_GROUP(...)
DMI_FIELD_OFFSET(offset)
----

== Description

The macros occupy no bytes, and say something about the fields after them,
see `DMI_FIELD`(3).

`DMI_FIELD_GROUP`::
Beginning of a group of fields the data may end before. The firmware writes
the structure of the version of the specification it follows, so a structure
is allowed to end at the beginning of any group: the fields of that group and
of the ones after it are left unset, and the structure is marked as partial
rather than broken. A structure which ends in the middle of a group is decoded
up to where it ends, and is marked as incomplete. The fields before the first
group are required.

`DMI_FIELD_OFFSET`::
Boundary the fields after it begin at, counted from the beginning of the
structure the way the tables of the specification count it. It says nothing
about where the data may end, and is checked while the structure is decoded
and encoded, so that a field left out of the declaration, or declared twice,
is caught where it happens rather than in the values of everything after it.

=== Parameters

`offset`::
Offset of the next field, from the beginning of the structure, header
included.

`...`::
Parameters of a group, given as the designated initializers of
`dmi_field_params_t`:
+
--
`.since`:::
Version of the specification the fields of the group appear in, which becomes
the level of the structure once the data reaches the group. Groups of the
vendors, whose fields no version brings, leave it unset.

`.present`:::
`bool` member, see `dmi_member`(3), set once every field of the group has been
read, and left `false` if the data ends before the group or in the middle of
it. Attributes tell the fields the structure holds from the ones it is too
short for by it, and encoding in the canonical mode writes no group the
structure does not hold.
--

== Errors

A boundary the fields do not reach exactly fails decoding and encoding with
`DMI_ERROR_INTERNAL`, which tells a declaration that does not match the data.

== Example

[source, c]
----
DMI_FIELD(dmi_cache_t, associativity, dmi_byte_t),

DMI_FIELD_GROUP(.since = DMI_VERSION(3, 1, 0)),
DMI_FIELD_EXTENDED(dmi_cache_t, maximum_size, dmi_dword_t, ...),
----

[source, c]
----
DMI_FIELD_GROUP(.present = dmi_member(dmi_hpe_trusted_module_t, has_chip)),
DMI_FIELD(dmi_hpe_trusted_module_t, chip, dmi_byte_t),
----
