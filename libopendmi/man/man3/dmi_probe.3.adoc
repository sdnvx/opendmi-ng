== Name

dmi_probe - voltage, temperature and electrical current probe structure

== Library

OpenDMI C library (`libopendmi`, `-lopendmi`)
endif::[]

== Synopsis

[source, c]
----
#include <opendmi/entity/probe.h>

typedef struct dmi_probe {
    const char *description;
    dmi_probe_location_t location;
    dmi_status_t status;
    short maximum_value;
    short minimum_value;
    short resolution;
    short tolerance;
    short accuracy;
    uint32_t oem_defined;
    short nominal_value;
} dmi_probe_t;

#include <opendmi/entity/voltage-probe.h>

typedef struct dmi_probe dmi_voltage_probe_t;

#include <opendmi/entity/temperature-probe.h>

typedef struct dmi_probe dmi_temperature_probe_t;

#include <opendmi/entity/current-probe.h>

typedef struct dmi_probe dmi_current_probe_t;
----

== Description

The `dmi_probe_t` structure holds a decoded voltage probe (type 26),
temperature probe (type 28) or electrical current probe (type 29) structure of
SMBIOS: a sensor of the system, where it is, its status, and the range and
precision of its readings. There is one structure for each probe. The three
types of structures are laid out the same way and differ only in the units of
their values, so they share the structure, which each of the headers of the
probes names after its type: `dmi_voltage_probe_t`, `dmi_temperature_probe_t`
and `dmi_current_probe_t`.

The values are in millivolts for voltage probes, in milliamperes for electrical
current probes, and in tenths of a degree Celsius for temperature probes,
unless said otherwise below. A management device component structure
(`dmi_mgmt_device_component_t`) may tie a probe to the management device
reading it, and to its thresholds.

The structure is returned by `dmi_entity_info`(3) for the structures of types
`DMI_TYPE(VOLTAGE_PROBE)`, `DMI_TYPE(TEMPERATURE_PROBE)` and
`DMI_TYPE(CURRENT_PROBE)`, and lives as long as the context it has been decoded
in.

=== Fields

`description`::
Additional description of the probe or of its location, or `nullptr` if there
is none.

`location`::
Physical location of the probe.

`status`::
Status of the voltage, temperature or current the probe monitors.

`maximum_value`::
Largest value the probe can read, or `SHRT_MIN` if unknown.

`minimum_value`::
Smallest value the probe can read, or `SHRT_MIN` if unknown.

`resolution`::
Resolution of the readings of the probe, or `SHRT_MIN` if unknown. The unit is
ten times as fine as the one of the other values for voltage and current probes
(tenths of a millivolt or of a milliampere), and a thousandth of a degree
Celsius for temperature probes.

`tolerance`::
Tolerance of the readings of the probe, plus or minus, or `SHRT_MIN` if
unknown.

`accuracy`::
Accuracy of the readings of the probe, plus or minus, in hundredths of a
percent, or `SHRT_MIN` if unknown.

`oem_defined`::
Information specific to the OEM or to the firmware vendor.

`nominal_value`::
Nominal reading of the probe, or `SHRT_MIN` if unknown or if the structure is
too short to carry it.

== Examples

[source, c]
----
dmi_registry_t *registry = dmi_get_registry(context);
dmi_entity_t   *entity   =
        dmi_registry_lookup_first(registry, DMI_TYPE(TEMPERATURE_PROBE),
                                  true);
dmi_probe_t    *probe    =
        dmi_entity_info(entity, DMI_TYPE(TEMPERATURE_PROBE));

if (probe != nullptr) {
    printf("%s: %s\n", dmi_probe_location_name(probe->location),
           dmi_status_name(probe->status));

    if (probe->nominal_value != SHRT_MIN)
        printf("Nominal: %.1f C\n", probe->nominal_value / 10.0);
}
----
