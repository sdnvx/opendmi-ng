= Intel Active Management Technology Information (Type 130)
:sectnums:
:sectlinks:

Source: Intel AMT Implementation and Reference Guide, "AMT Information Table
Parameter Definitions"; MeshAgent, `modules/smbios.js`; and the dumps of the
data corpus.

Tells whether the platform supports Intel Active Management Technology (AMT),
and which of its features are enabled. The structure is told by the `$AMT`
signature at offset `0x04`.

The guide gives the flags of the features as 0 when disabled, 1 when enabled,
and values from 2 on as not applicable, and puts a structure version of two
bytes after the signature, which no firmware of the data corpus holds. The
flags are not consistent with each other on every platform, e.g. some give
the network as enabled while AMT is not supported, so they are best taken as
the firmware reports them rather than as the state of the platform.

| `0x0F`
| 2.0+
| OEM Capabilities 2
| `BYTE`
| `ENUM`

| `0x10`
| 2.0+
| OEM Capabilities 3
| `BYTE`
| `BITFIELD`
| Bits 0 to 5 are reserved, bit 6: Remote Secure Erase is supported, bit 7: the BIOS supports Secure Boot

| `0x11`
| 2.0+
| OEM Capabilities 4
| `BYTE`
| `BITFIELD`
| Targets of the remote boot the platform supports, see below

| `0x12`
| 2.0+
| KVM Enabled
| `BYTE`
| `BOOLEAN`
| Keyboard, video and mouse redirection

| `0x13`
| 2.0+
| Reserved
| `BYTE`
| `0`
|

| `0x14`

| Reserved

.OEM capabilities 1
[width=100%, cols="1,4"]
|===
| Bits | Meaning

| 0
| Storage redirection is supported

| 1
| Serial over LAN is supported

| 2
| The BIOS can be reflashed remotely

| 3
| The setup of the BIOS can be entered remotely

| 4
| The BIOS can be paused remotely

| 5
| Booting from the floppy disk can be prevented

| 6
| Booting from the CD can be prevented

| 7
| Reserved, while most firmware of the data corpus sets it
|===
