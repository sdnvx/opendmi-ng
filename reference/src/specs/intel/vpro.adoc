= Intel vPro Information (Type 131)
:sectnums:
:sectlinks:

Source: reverse engineered from the dumps of the data corpus, whose versions
match the ones of Intel firmware version information (type 221).

Describes the parts of the platform Intel vPro technology relies on: the
versions of the Management Engine firmware and of its BIOS extension (MEBx),
and the PCI functions of the chipset and of the network controller the
Management Engine uses. The structure is found on Intel platforms whether
they are of vPro or not.

The structure is told by the `vPro` signature at offset `0x38`, since some
vendors give type 131 to structures of their own in the same table, e.g.
Lenovo ThinkVantage Technologies enablement. PCI functions are given as their
device and function numbers on bus 0, `(device << 3) | function`.
