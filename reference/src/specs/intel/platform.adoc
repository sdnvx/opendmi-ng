= Intel Platform Information (Type 148)
:sectnums:
:sectlinks:

Source: Intel Bay Trail reference code, `Vlv2TbltDevicePkg`, as EDK II
published it up to edk2-stable201903: the OEM type `0x94` of
`SmBiosMiscDxe`.

Gives the versions of the firmware components and some settings of an Intel
Bay Trail platform, all of them as strings: the numbers the firmware writes
in hexadecimal, and the settings as their values, e.g. `0` or `1`. The data
corpus holds it on Lenovo IdeaPad 100S, whose firmware is of Insyde.

The structure is told by its length of 26 bytes, since Intel server boards
give type 148 to structures of other layouts, e.g. S2600WTT to one of 48
bytes, which gives the versions of the firmware components of the board.

| 2.0+
| Type

| Intel Platform Information indicator

| 2.0+
| Length
| `BYTE`
| `0x1A`
| Length of the structure

| 2.0+
| Handle
| `WORD`
| Varies

| `0x04`
| 2.0+
| GOP Version
| `BYTE`
| `STRING`
| Version of the graphics output protocol (GOP) driver, e.g. `7.2.1013`

| `0x05`
| 2.0+
| Microcode Version
| `BYTE`
| `STRING`
| Revision of the microcode in hexadecimal, e.g. `832`

| `0x06`
| 2.0+
| MRC Version
| `BYTE`
| `STRING`
| Version of the memory reference code (MRC), e.g. `1.02`

| `0x07`
| 2.0+
| SEC Version
| `BYTE`
| `STRING`
| Version of the firmware of the security engine, the Trusted Execution Engine (TXE), e.g. `1.2.0.1149`

| `0x08`
| 2.0+
| ULPMC Version
| `BYTE`
| `STRING`
| Version of the firmware of the ultra low power microcontroller (ULPMC), or `Non ULPMC!!` if there is none

| `0x09`
| 2.0+
| PMC Version
| `BYTE`
| `STRING`
| Version of the firmware of the power management controller (PMC), e.g. `0x4_45`

| `0x0A`
| 2.0+
| P-unit Version
| `BYTE`
| `STRING`
| Version of the firmware of the P-unit, e.g. `0x27`

| `0x0B`
| 2.0+
| SoC Version
| `BYTE`
| `STRING`
| Stepping of the system on a chip, e.g. `0F (C0 Stepping)`

| `0x0C`
| 2.0+
| Board Version
| `BYTE`
| `STRING`
| Board, e.g. `BAY LAKE CR (6)`

| `0x0D`
| 2.0+
| Fab Version
| `BYTE`
| `STRING`
| Fab of the board, e.g. `0`

| `0x0E`
| 2.0+
| CPU Flavor
| `BYTE`
| `STRING`
| Flavor of the processor, e.g. `VLV-QC Notebook (3)`

| `0x0F`
| 2.0+
| BIOS Version
| `BYTE`
| `STRING`
| Version of the firmware, e.g. `E2CN13WW`

| `0x10`
| 2.0+
| PMIC Version
| `BYTE`
| `STRING`
| Vendor and revision of the power management IC (PMIC), e.g. `41.01`

| `0x11`
| 2.0+
| Touch Version
| `BYTE`
| `STRING`
| Version of the firmware of the touch screen, `NA` if there is none

| `0x12`
| 2.0+
| Secure Boot
| `BYTE`
| `STRING`
| Secure Boot setting, `0` or `1`

| `0x13`
| 2.0+
| Boot Mode
| `BYTE`
| `STRING`
| Boot mode of the firmware, as a number

| `0x14`
| 2.0+
| SpeedStep Mode
| `BYTE`
| `STRING`
| Enhanced Intel SpeedStep setting

| `0x15`
| 2.0+
| Turbo Mode
| `BYTE`
| `STRING`
| Turbo Boost setting

| `0x16`
| 2.0+
| Maximum C-state
| `BYTE`
| `STRING`
| Deepest C-state of the processor, e.g. `3`

| `0x17`
| 2.0+
| Graphics Turbo
| `BYTE`
| `STRING`
| Turbo setting of the graphics

| `0x18`
| 2.0+
| Idle Reserve
| `BYTE`
| `STRING`
| S0ix setting

| `0x19`
| 2.0+
| RC6
| `BYTE`
| `STRING`
| RC6 power saving setting of the graphics
