= Intel Silicon View Technology Milestones (Type 222)
:sectnums:
:sectlinks:

Source: reverse engineered from the dumps of the data corpus.

Lists the milestones of the boot the firmware reports to the debug and
validation tools of Intel Silicon View Technology (SVT). A group association
(type 14) named `Intel(R) Silicon View Technology` often refers to the
structure. The reference code gives the structure type 222, which Dell places
at type 206, while HP servers carry none.

[#intel-svt-aligned, reftext=Aligned layout]
== Intel Silicon View Technology Milestones -- Aligned Layout

Some firmware does not pack the structure, e.g. the one of Dell XPS 13 9365,
which carries `01 00 99 00 03 10 01 20 02 30 03 00` where other firmware
carries `01 99 00 03 10 01 20 02 30 03`: the parameter is aligned to two
bytes, and the milestones are followed by a byte which makes the length of
the structure even. The structure is told by the byte of zero at `0x05`,
where the packed layout holds the low byte of the parameter.
