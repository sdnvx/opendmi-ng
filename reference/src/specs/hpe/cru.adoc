= HP/HPE 64-bit CRU Information (Type 212)
:sectnums:
:sectlinks:

Source: dmidecode, `dmioem.c`, the `hpwdt` watchdog timer driver of Linux, and the dumps of the data corpus.

Tells where the 64-bit entry point of the Compaq ROM Utility (CRU) services of
the firmware is, which the watchdog timer driver calls. The structure is known
up to Gen8. Servers of AMD carry a record of another signature too, `$SHF`,
whose meaning is not known: dmidecode and the watchdog timer driver read the
rest of the fields of the records of the `$CRU` signature only.
