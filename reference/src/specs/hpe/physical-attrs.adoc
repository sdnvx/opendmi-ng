= HP/HPE Physical Attribute Information (Type 226)
:sectnums:
:sectlinks:

Source: dmidecode, `dmioem.c`, and the dumps of the data corpus.

Keeps the physical serial number and UUID of the server, whose standard fields
hold virtual ones, e.g. on Synergy, where a workload moves from one server to
another along with its identity.

Up to G7, the servers of the data corpus hold 16 ASCII characters instead of
the UUID: the product number and the serial number, e.g. `484184GB894484YN`.
