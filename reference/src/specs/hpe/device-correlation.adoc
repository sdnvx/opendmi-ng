= HP/HPE Device Correlation Record (Type 203)
:sectnums:
:sectlinks:

Source: dmidecode, `dmioem.c`. No dumps of the data corpus hold the structure.

Correlates a device with the system slot (type 9) or the onboard device (type
41) it is found at, and with the UEFI boot entries naming it. The structure is
decoded from Gen9 onwards. It has grown over the generations, and the shortest
structures end after the device name, at `0x1F`. The PCI location, from
`0x24` on, is present in the structures of 40 bytes or more.
