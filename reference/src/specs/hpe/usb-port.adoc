= HP/HPE USB Port Connector Correlation Record (Type 238)
:sectnums:
:sectlinks:

Source: dmidecode, `dmioem.c`. No dumps of the data corpus hold the structure.

Correlates a USB port connector (type 8) with its USB controller, its location
and the UEFI device path of its endpoint. The structure is decoded from Gen9
onwards.
