= HP/HPE Hard Drive Inventory Record (Type 242)
:sectnums:
:sectlinks:

Source: dmidecode, `dmioem.c`. No dumps of the data corpus hold the structure.

Describes an NVMe or SATA drive attached to the system directly, which the
firmware has found at boot. Drives behind a storage controller, e.g. a Smart
Array one, and the ones plugged in later are not described. The structure is
decoded from Gen10 onwards, and the shortest structures end after the firmware
revision, at `0x2A`.
