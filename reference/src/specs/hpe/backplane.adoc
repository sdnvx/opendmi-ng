= HP/HPE HDD Backplane FRU Information (Type 236)
:sectnums:
:sectlinks:

Source: dmidecode, `dmioem.c`. No dumps of the data corpus hold the structure.

Describes a drive backplane: where its FRU is, and the SAS expander and the
drive bays it has. The structure is decoded up to Gen10 Plus, from which the
bay counts behind the ports and the name are deprecated. The shortest
structures end after the NVRAM ID, at `0x09`.
