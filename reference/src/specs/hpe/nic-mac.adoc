= HP/HPE NIC PCI and MAC Information (Type 233)
:sectnums:
:sectlinks:

Source: dmidecode, `dmioem.c`. No dumps of the data corpus hold the structure.

Describes a network port the firmware boots from over PXE, one per structure.
The shortest structures end after the first 6 bytes of the MAC address, at
`0x0E`. A port is disabled if its bus and device are zero, and not installed
if they have all bits set.
