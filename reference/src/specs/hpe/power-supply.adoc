= HP/HPE Power Supply Information (Type 230)
:sectnums:
:sectlinks:

Source: dmidecode, `dmioem.c`, and the dumps of the data corpus.

Supplements the system power supply information (type 39) with the actual
manufacturer of the power supply and the way its FRU is accessed.

.HP/HPE Power Supply Information structure
[width=100%, cols="1,1,2,1,1,4"]
|===
| Offset | Version | Name | Length | Value | Description

| `0x00`
| 2.0+
| Type
| `BYTE`
| `230`
| HP/HPE Power Supply Information indicator

| `0x01`
| 2.0+
| Length
| `BYTE`
| `0x0B`
| Length of the structure

| `0x02`
| 2.0+
| Handle
| `WORD`
| Varies
| 

| `0x04`
| 2.0+
| Power Supply Handle
| `WORD`
| Varies
| Handle of the system power supply structure (type 39)

| `0x06`
| 2.0+
| Manufacturer
| `BYTE`
| `STRING`
| Actual manufacturer

| `0x07`
| 2.0+
| Revision
| `BYTE`
| `STRING`
| 

| `0x08`
| 2.0+
| FRU Access Method
| `BYTE`
| `ENUM`
| `0`: not available, `1`: IPMI I2C, `2`: iLO, `3`: chassis manager

| `0x09`
| 2.0+
| I2C Bus
| `BYTE`
| `NUMBER`
| I2C bus, or I2C segment with iLO, `0xFF` if not available

| `0x0A`
| 2.0+
| I2C Address
| `BYTE`
| `NUMBER`
| I2C address shifted left by one bit, `0xFF` if not available
|===
