= HP/HPE Processor TControl Information (Type 211)
:sectnums:
:sectlinks:

Source: dmidecode, `dmioem.c`, and the dumps of the data corpus.

Gives the Tcontrol value of a processor, which Intel programs into each
processor after the thermal properties of its transistors. The servers begin
to spin up the fans at this temperature. The processor information structure
(type 4) tells whether the processor is installed.
