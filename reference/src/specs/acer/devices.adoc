= Acer Device List (Type 171)
:sectnums:
:sectlinks:

Source: reverse engineered from the dumps of the data corpus.

Lists the devices of a laptop by their PCI or USB vendor and device IDs.
Devices run to the end of the structure, which carries no number of them of
its own, and the absent ones have zero IDs.

In `acer/nitro-an515-31.bin`, the kinds match the devices as follows: `1`
Intel UHD Graphics 620 (`8086:5917`), `2` and `3` NVIDIA GeForce MX150
(`10DE:1D10`), `4` Realtek webcam (`0BDA:5621`), `5` Intel audio (`8086:9D71`)
and `7` Intel Wi-Fi 6E AX210 (`8086:2725`).
