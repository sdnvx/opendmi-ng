= AMI FireWire GUID (Type 139)
:sectnums:
:sectlinks:

Source: reverse engineered from the dumps of the data corpus.

Keeps the data holding the GUID of the IEEE 1394 (FireWire) controller of the
board, under the name `V1394GUID`. The data corpus holds a single structure of
the kind, on ASUS M5A97 PRO, so the layout of the data is not established:
it begins like the bus information block of the configuration ROM of IEEE
1394, with the lengths of the block and of its CRC, `04 04`, followed by the
CRC and the options of the bus, and the OUI of VIA, `00 40 63`, is found
further on.

The structure is told by its signature at offset `0x04` and by its length.
The data identifies the board, and is replaced when the table is anonymized.
