= Dell BIOS Flags (Type 177)
:sectnums:
:sectlinks:

Source: the Dell SMBIOS WMI driver of Linux, `dell-smbios-wmi.c`, dmidecode, `dmioem.c`, and the dumps of the data corpus.

Tells the features of the firmware the Dell drivers rely on, of which only the
support of ACPI WMI is known. dmidecode reads the flags as a `QWORD`, and the
Dell SMBIOS WMI driver of Linux as a `WORD`; the bytes past the first word are
not known to carry anything. The driver reads the structures of 12 bytes and
of handle `0xB100` only, which every structure of the data corpus is.
