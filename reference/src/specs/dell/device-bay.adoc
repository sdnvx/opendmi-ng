= Dell Device Bay (Type 219)
:sectnums:
:sectlinks:

Source: reverse engineered from the dumps of the data corpus.

Describes a bay of a laptop or of its docking station, which takes one of
several devices, e.g. `System Device Bay`, which takes `Floppy, Battery,
CD-ROM, CD-RW, Hard Disk, DVD` and holds `Battery` in `dell/latitude-d500.bin`,
or `Dock DBay`, which holds `EMPTY` in `dell/latitude-d610.bin`.

Newer platforms give the structure 11 bytes, which refer to two more strings
whose meaning is not established: Latitude E6230 has a bay and leaves them
out, while Precision M3800 and G15 5515, which have none, carry a structure of
blank strings or of `00h`. XPS 13 9350 gives type 219 to the Intel Management
Engine interface information, which is told by its signature, and the
structure is decoded for the other structures of the type. The numbers of the
strings do not tell it, since the structures whose strings are the same are
written back with a single one.
