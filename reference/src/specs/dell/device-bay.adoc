= Dell Device Bay (Type 219)
:sectnums:
:sectlinks:

Source: reverse engineered from the dumps of the data corpus.

Describes a bay of a laptop or of its docking station, which takes one of
several devices, e.g. `System Device Bay`, which takes `Floppy, Battery,
CD-ROM, CD-RW, Hard Disk, DVD` and holds `Battery` in `dell/latitude-d500.bin`,
or `Dock DBay`, which holds `EMPTY` in `dell/latitude-d610.bin`.

Other Dell platforms give type 219 to structures of other layouts, e.g.
Precision M3800 to one of 11 bytes referring to five blank strings, and XPS 13
9350 to one of 81 bytes referring to the strings `MEI1` to `MEI3`, so the
structure is told by its length of 9 bytes.
