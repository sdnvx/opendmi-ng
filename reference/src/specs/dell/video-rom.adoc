= Dell Video BIOS Information (Type 216)
:sectnums:
:sectlinks:

Source: reverse engineered from the dumps of the data corpus.

Tells the vendor and the version of the video BIOS, e.g. `"Intel Corp."` and
`"2089"` in `dell/g15-5510.bin`, or `ATI` and `RADEON 7000 V6.11` in
`dell/poweredge-1800.bin`.
