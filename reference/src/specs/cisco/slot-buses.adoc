= Cisco PCI Slot Buses (Type 201)
:sectnums:
:sectlinks:

Source: reverse engineered from the dumps of the data corpus.

Lists the PCI buses behind a slot of a Cisco UCS server, presumably. The
numbers match the adapters the PCI adapter information (type 202) gives for
the same slot: an adapter with a PCI switch, e.g. Cisco UCS VIC 1455, has
several buses, and the other adapters one.
