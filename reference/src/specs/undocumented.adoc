= Undocumented structures
:sectnums:
:sectlinks:

Structures found in the dumps of the data corpus which no public source
describes, and whose meaning could not be established from the data. They are
left undecoded, and what is known of them is gathered here, so that the
dumps which will tell more can be recognized.

== Structures of several vendors

=== Type 128: sample structure of the firmware kit

Found on Insyde firmware: Lenovo IdeaPad and ThinkBook, Toshiba Satellite,
Gigabyte G5 and Neousys Nuvo.

The formatted area holds the bytes `55 AA 55 AA`, and the structure carries
the strings `Oem Test 1` and `Oem Test 2`, or `Oem Type 128 Test 1` and
`Oem Type 128 Test 2`, which no field refers to. The structure is a sample
of the firmware kit which the vendors of the systems leave in place.

=== Type 129: Intel ASF

Found on Intel platforms of Insyde and Phoenix firmware: HP EliteBook,
Lenovo IdeaPad, ThinkBook and ThinkPad, Toshiba Satellite, Gigabyte G5 and
Neousys Nuvo. Commonly listed as the Intel Alert Standard Format (ASF)
structure.

[width=100%, cols="1,1,4"]
|===
| Offset | Length | Value

| `0x04`
| `BYTE`
| `1` in all known data

| `0x05`
| `BYTE`
| String `1`: `Intel_ASF`, or `Insyde_ASF_001` on Insyde firmware

| `0x06`
| `BYTE`
| String `2`: `Intel_ASF_001`, or `Insyde_ASF_002`

| `0x07`
| `BYTE`
| `1`, or `0` on ThinkPad T61p. It does not follow the state of Intel AMT
  the type 130 structure of the same table tells.
|===

Lenovo IdeaPad Z565 carries a structure of type 129 of 5 bytes, whose strings
are the ones of a type 128 sample, cut short.

=== Type 133

Lenovo firmware of all the known models, from ThinkPad T61p to ThinkPad T14
Gen 3, carries a structure of 5 bytes, which refers to its only string at `0x04`: `KHOIHGIUCCHHII`, the same
in all known data.

AMI firmware of Intel platforms of the 12th generation and later (ASUS Prime
H610M-R, Gigabyte B760M DS3H, Beelink SEi) carries a structure of 44 bytes,
identical on all of them, which begins with the GUID
`7895B4F9-924D-410A-9639-123862B28A63` and holds the PCI vendor ID of Intel,
`0x8086`, twice. ThinkPad T14 Gen 3 carries one of the same GUID with other
bytes and strings, and ASUS RS520-E9 one of 12 bytes.

=== Type 136

Found on the firmware of every vendor, mostly on Intel platforms. The
formatted area holds a single `WORD`, whose value depends on the code base of
the firmware rather than on the system: `0x0000` on AMI firmware, `0xFFFF` on
Insyde firmware, and `0x5A5A` on the firmware of Phoenix code base, e.g.
ThinkPad, Dell Precision 490 and HP Z600. Newer AMI tables carry a second
structure of type 136, of a `DWORD` of zero, which is the only one of Dell G15
5510.

=== Type 143

Fujitsu LifeBook S 4546 carries two structures, the first of which holds the
signature `_FJ_OEM_` at `0x05`. MSI PRO B650-S WIFI carries one referring to
eleven strings, which name the firmware components and their versions, e.g.
`GOP1-[N/A]`, `RAID-1...16`, `PXEU1-v.2`, `AMI-AptioV` and `MsiFlash-17`.

=== Type 248

Acer Nitro AN515-31 carries `00 02 6C 00 00 00 00`, and Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5
and ThinkBook 15 G2 carry 14 bytes beginning with `AC AC AA 17`.

== Acer

=== Type 172

Acer Nitro AN515-31 carries `02 36`, followed by 3-byte records of an ID,
from `1` to `7`, and a `WORD` value: `0x00FF`, `1`, `0x00FF`, `1`, `0x000F`,
`0x00FF` and `0x00FF`. The IDs are the kinds of the devices of the device list
(type 171), and `0x36` is the length of that structure, which may be a
coincidence.

== Dell

=== Type 179

Dell G15 5510 carries a structure referring to a single string, `1[` followed
by 64 hexadecimal digits and `]`, in the `key[value]` form of the OEM strings
(type 11) of Dell. The digits are as many as the ones of a SHA-256 hash.

=== Type 217

Dell G15 5510 carries a structure referring to the strings `US-101` and
`Proprietary` at `0x04` and `0x05`, followed by `01 03`, which may describe
the keyboard.

=== Type 222

Found on Dell PowerEdge 1800, Precision 490 and G15 5510, of 15 or 16 bytes,
e.g. `01 04 FF FF` followed by zeros, or `C1 01 FF FF` followed by zeros and
`01`.

== HP

=== ProLiant servers

The ProLiant G1 to G6 servers carry structures of types 196, 198, 205, 210,
213, 214, 215, 217, 218, 220, 222, 223, 227 and 228, which neither dmidecode
nor the drivers of HP describe. Type 196 holds a single byte of zero, type
198 e.g. `17 00 00 01 3C 00 01`, and type 205 the ASCII text `FATx` at `0x06`.

=== HP Z600

The workstation carries structures of types 153, 197 and 207, of which type
197 is not the processor information of the ProLiant servers.

== Lenovo

=== Type 131: CMOS layout

ThinkPad T61p carries a structure of type 131 referring to the strings
`BOOTINF 20h`, `BOOTDEV 21h` and `KEYPTRS 23h`, which name registers of the
CMOS memory, along with the Intel vPro information and the ThinkVantage
Technologies enablement of the same type.

=== Type 132

ThinkPads and Lenovo B590 carry `02 D8 36`, `01 D8 36` or `02 DA 36`,
sometimes followed by a byte of zero.

=== Types 137 to 139 and 141

ThinkPad T61p carries structures of types 137 to 139, and ThinkPad T14 Gen 2a
and Gen 3 structures of type 141 of 18 to 56 bytes.

== Foxconn

Foxconn M61PMV, of Phoenix firmware, carries structures of types 188, 190, 192
and 194, of 212 to 244 bytes, which hold tables of small numbers.
