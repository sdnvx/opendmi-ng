= Lenovo TPM Information (Type 134)
:sectnums:
:sectlinks:

Source: reverse engineered from the dumps of the data corpus.

Tells the vendor of the TPM of a Lenovo laptop by its TCG vendor ID, e.g. `ATML`
for Atmel in `lenovo/thinkpad-t61p-6460-6xg.bin`, or `STM ` for
STMicroelectronics in `lenovo/thinkpad-x220-4290le6.bin`. The structures are
told from the date records of the same type by their first string, `TPM INFO`.
