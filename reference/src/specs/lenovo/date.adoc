= Lenovo Date Record (Type 134)
:sectnums:
:sectlinks:

Source: reverse engineered from the dumps of the data corpus.

Holds a date of a ThinkPad, which falls within the years the model has been made
in, and so is probably its manufacture date, e.g. November 27, 2021 in
`lenovo/thinkpad-t14-g2a-20xk.bin`. Structures of type 134 holding TPM
information instead are told by their first string, `TPM INFO`.
