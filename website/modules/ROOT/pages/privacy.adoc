= Privacy
:navtitle: Privacy

This page tells what this site learns about its visitors, and how a visitor
decides on it. The site is published by the OpenDMI contributors, who are
reached at mailto:dsedneff@gmail.com[dsedneff@gmail.com] for anything about
the data of the visitors, and through the {url-repo}/issues[issues of the
project] on GitHub for anything else.

== Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub keeps logs of the requests made to
the sites it hosts, including the IP addresses of the visitors, to keep its
service secure, as the
https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement[GitHub
General Privacy Statement] describes. The OpenDMI contributors have no access
to these logs.

Everything the pages are made of, the fonts, the styles and the scripts, is
served by the site itself, so opening a page makes no requests to any other
site.

== Analytics

The site counts its visits with Google Analytics, only if the visitor accepts
its cookies: until then, the script of Google Analytics is not loaded at all.

Once accepted, Google Analytics learns the pages visited, the page the visitor
has come from, the browser, the device and the approximate location, which it
derives from the IP address without storing the address itself. The OpenDMI
contributors see these in aggregate, as numbers of visits, and use them to
learn which parts of the documentation are read, and nothing else. Google
processes the data as the https://policies.google.com/privacy[Google Privacy
Policy] describes.

Google Analytics sets two cookies, which are kept for up to two years:

`_ga`::
Tells visits of the same browser apart from visits of other ones.

`_ga_` followed by the identifier of the site::
Keeps the state of the current visit.

The data is processed on the basis of the consent of the visitor.

== Your choice

The site asks for the choice the first time it is opened, and remembers it in
the browser, in its local storage rather than in a cookie, so that it is not
asked for again. The choice is sent nowhere.

The choice is changed at any time through the *Cookie settings* link at the
foot of every page. Declining after accepting stops Google Analytics and
removes its cookies.

Visitors have the rights the data protection laws give them, such as to
access the data held about them and to have it erased, which they exercise by
writing to mailto:dsedneff@gmail.com[dsedneff@gmail.com], and to lodge a
complaint with a data protection authority.
